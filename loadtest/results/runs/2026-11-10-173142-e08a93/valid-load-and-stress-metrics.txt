LIMITATIONS
- Whole-run summaries include ramps and graceful completion, not only peak-load holds.
- Hold windows are approximate, anchored to the first HTTP Point in the raw file. That sample may occur after scheduled execution starts, and concurrent samples may arrive out of timestamp order.
- Hold latency samples belong to the window containing their completion timestamp, including requests that started before the window.
- http_req_failed follows k6 expected-response classification, not exclusively HTTP 5xx. JSON/body checks are separate and have no configured checks threshold.
- In http_req_failed, passes counts TRUE failure observations and fails counts FALSE observations. In checks, passes instead counts successful predicates.
- Zero failed observed checks is reported as a descriptive correctness verdict, not a newly configured k6 threshold.
- Diagnostic timing, transfer, throughput, iteration and VU metrics have no pass/fail acceptance threshold in these scripts.
- All timing values are milliseconds. Counter rates use the handleSummary testRunDurationMs, not first-to-last request time.
- Raw percentiles use linear interpolation at (n-1)*p; authoritative k6 summary values are retained separately for reconciliation.
- Measurements cover public listing reads through the gateway, including downstream calls. They do not establish booking, payment or write performance, nor identify a bottleneck without resource and service evidence.
